## Reproduction and archive boundary

The current [offline command and dependencies](../results/evaluation/rev3_consolidated_20261013/README.md#offline-reproduction) reproduce the compact tables, report and figures in a fresh output directory. [Validation](../results/evaluation/rev3_consolidated_20261013/validation/README.md) distinguishes this from the private B-only extraction/restoration test on Twix.

The canonical private archive uses `diss_results/CURRENT.json` and a dated `_supplements/native_fairness_rev3_20261013` addition. Earlier originals and the source archive remain intact. The private archive retains large raw traces, models and original reports; public reproduction does not reintegrate traces, rerun datasets or recreate SDK/runtime environments. The unresolved historical Full-producer source version (21 references), 17 direct administrative receipt-version gaps and broader unresolved provenance branches are documented without claiming complete raw replay.

Private manuscript work is not published here. Historical STOP/resume notes are evidence of their original state and do not authorize a measurement campaign.
