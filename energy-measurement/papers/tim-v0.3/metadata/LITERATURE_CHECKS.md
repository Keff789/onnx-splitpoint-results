# Literature use in this revision

The related-work framing was rewritten around experimental comparability,
calibration versus temporal resolution, and telemetry polling versus physical
sampling. Bibliographic entries come from the supplied TIM source; the
companion manuscript and evidence citations were updated to the actual
PARMA v0.15.1 author-manuscript/repository state. No acceptance or DOI was
invented. The separate pause evidence was added as a pinned repository source.

Primary sources consulted for interpretation during the revision:

- Shalavi et al., external calibration of Jetson sensors:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13107
- Gralka et al., aggregate versus temporal measurement and synchronisation:
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12650-024-01001-0
- Dauner et al., RAPL/NVML polling-interval experiment:
  https://hotcarbon.org/assets/2026/paper-46.pdf

These checks inform the positioning of the existing references; external
papers do not replace this project's numerical evidence, supply missing
hardware settings or validate the upcoming GPU campaign. The supplied
bibliography's remaining entries are retained, not claimed to have undergone
a new complete systematic literature review.
