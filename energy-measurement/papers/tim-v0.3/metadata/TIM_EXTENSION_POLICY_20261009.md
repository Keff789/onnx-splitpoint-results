# Official TIM author guidance — checked 9 October 2026

Source: IEEE Instrumentation and Measurement Society, *Information for Authors*.
https://ieee-ims.org/publication/ieee-tim/information-authors
Sections: Paper Guidelines; Extended Version of Proceedings Paper; Special Section in TIM.

TIM permits technically extended proceedings papers as regular papers after the
conference closes. It requests an explanatory cover letter, a technical extension
list, the proceedings copy, citation and an introductory reuse declaration, with
applicable permissions. More explanation alone is not a technical extension.
The manuscript must be self-contained; supplementary material cannot replace
information essential to understanding it. Regular papers have no stated maximum
length, but overlength charges apply.
