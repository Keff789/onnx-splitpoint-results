# Data selection and statistical methods

The selected generation is the completed bounded Revision 3 delta of 13 October 2026. Its 246 Native and 204 Generic rows remain separate from the broader historical THESIS20 data. The archived Current JSONs are the selection authority; exact report bindings and unresolved provenance branches are tracked separately; chronological filename order and favorable values are not selection rules.

Each identity includes runner regime, model, actual setup, backend and split/full case. Selection additionally checks source cohort hashes, numerical input, completed-task boundary, endpoint and quality joins. `cohort_actions.json` records every replacement of an old completed-task result selection. Old measurements are preserved. Original raw-endpoint Generic results, all 560 historical quality observations, unsupported cases, build/policy negatives and the separate diagnostic acceptance retain their own cohorts.

Performance retains the stored median of three independent processes. Six historical Native Full observations remain singles. Energy uses the arithmetic mean of each sensor repeat's E/N. Efficiency retains mean(N/E), and the reciprocal of mean(E/N) and pooled sum(N)/sum(E) are separately labelled. Tables retain unrounded machine values; the report displays integer FPS and mJ/task.

The normal energy projection defines admissibility. Native/Generic joins require current exact technical gates and exact Native summary and Generic result hashes. Generic calibration, physical scope and window are read from its own bound energy aggregates, not inherited from Native. Functional numerical parity is distinct from dataset accuracy or mAP.

Full comparisons require both the archived semantic and claim flags. Energy may be admitted for a historical Full performance single. Performance comparisons additionally require both performance claims and are deliberately limited to the subset with an available strict energy-pair contract. This conservative subset must not be described as every conceivable admissible performance comparison. All 204 split identities appear in baseline selection; absent contracts remain missing. Fastest Full and lowest-energy Full are selected independently from each split's admissible candidates.

Raw calibrated full-system energy and the existing verified host normalization are separate. No new idle subtraction is applied. Quality losses are retained; the reference-close-only analysis is an explicit sensitivity filter. Pair observations sharing a Full baseline are dependent, and counts of pairs are not independent sample sizes.

Pareto sets are two-dimensional (FPS maximized, raw J/task minimized) within exact model/setup/runner and measurement-contract groups; quality is reported and filtered separately. Graph features are bound to the exact original model hashes and index-verified analysis files. Within-group rank correlations are descriptive and post hoc. No significance or causal claim is made.

Pipeline fields come only from SHA-bound Current raw performance reports. Worker times overlap; H10 asynchronous latency is not its throughput cycle, queue wait is not handoff work, and postprocessing can alias the completion tail. No synthetic additive percentages are computed. Groups retain the producer implementation where it is recorded.

Energy confidence intervals use the existing two-sided Student t method at 95%, n=3 and df=2. They estimate a mean, not future single observations. FPS ranges preserve the median estimand. All nine cross-products of two three-repeat cohorts are unpaired empirical sensitivity checks; they are not nine independent replicates. No CI is invented for single observations. No random algorithm or new bootstrap seed is used.

The YOLOv7/DeepX diagnostic acceptance single is analyzed separately against its historical descriptive context. It does not extend the scientific cohort. The missing historical Full-producer source hash with 21 references remains an explicit provenance limit. Compact reproduction does not reintegrate sensor traces or recreate dataset-quality evaluations.

Seventeen direct administrative Native MobileNet/DeepX join receipts have an unavailable expected byte version. Their existing receipt alternatives and expected hashes are retained; the normal result and validation-summary leaves match Current exactly in all 56 examined direct joins. This does not close the 17 administrative version gaps. Associated Generic comparisons are flagged, and an explicit sensitivity excludes these identities. The all-admitted analysis reports inherited eligibility flags without claiming a complete provenance chain.

The baseline sensitivity reselects the fastest and lowest-energy admissible Full per split for each quality/provenance filter and raw/comparison-energy basis; model/setup split optima are then reselected as well. Baseline changes, direction changes and lost comparison coverage are distinct outputs.
