# Offline comparison protocol (post hoc, 2026-10-05)

This analysis evaluates existing selector definitions; it does not fit or recalibrate them. The canonical source remains the adjacent `thesis20_20261004` projection and `deep_analysis`. New compact method scores are supplied in `inputs/method_candidate_scores.csv`; none replaces an original measurement.

Primary comparisons use identical measured candidates within each original model/setup/direction/precision/comparison-backend/endpoint/boundary stratum. Technical, original quality-transfer and stricter reference-close eligibility retain their existing meanings. A method with missing scores is displayed as incomplete, with no full-cohort performance metric. Historical raw Generic is evaluated only in the exact original base intersection. All groups with fewer than three eligible candidates remain visible, with insufficient-group status.

Spearman and Kendall tau-b reuse the released pure ranking functions. Selection uses the method's stored original order for exact score ties; tie-sensitive loss bounds are additional. Native target ties use original completion-pair order. Throughput loss is `L_R=1-R_selected/R_best`; capacity/cycle regret is `L_C=R_best/R_selected-1`. Neither represents measured single-request latency. Top-k tables separately report Native-best recall and overlap with the stable Native Top-k. Uniform random subsets without replacement provide exact analytical probabilities and expected losses. They are not new measured runs. Top3 at n=3 is labelled trivial and excluded from method-success summaries.

Per-repeat sensitivity uses the three existing Native repeats. Measured Generic completion also has three predictor repeats, giving nine dependent combinations; static and raw aggregate predictors give three Native combinations. Energy uses independent existing energy windows: per-replicate E/N, means of the three original ratios, never separate performance FPS as denominator. The selected candidate's energy excess is `J_selected/min(J)-1`; an additional bounded lost-savings fraction uses `1-min(J)/J_selected`. Energy repetition ranges and selection changes are descriptive and are not confidence intervals. No Full baseline is needed or duplicated.

Global graph-universe recommendations and their measured/unsupported status are a separate inventory. No unmeasured candidate is assigned a performance, energy or quality result, and no automatic fallback to a measured candidate is claimed as the original global recommendation.

The fixed-selection energy sensitivity compares the three energy repetitions of the aggregate-selected identity against the three repetitions of the aggregate energy optimum (nine dependent ratios). If these are the same identity, the primary selection penalty is exactly zero; off-diagonal repetition ratios describe variation of that same case and are explicitly labelled `same_case_repeat_ratio_variation_not_selection_penalty`. They must not be presented as selection loss. Separate tables recompute the energy optimum by repetition index; equal indices do not imply synchronized acquisition.
