# THESIS20: findings from the completed campaign

The evidence supports a completed **technical measurement inventory** and descriptive comparisons. It does not establish a controlled causal hardware comparison or held-out selector accuracy. Existing Cross-Runner and energy comparison claim gates remain false.

1. **Coverage is complete within the documented scope.** The original Generic matrix contains 527 measured observations, 37 build failures and 24 policy exclusions (588 required cases). Quality contains 548 original plus 12 added results: 521 `reference_close`, 39 `accuracy_loss`, all read with N=5000/B=1000 and seed 20260710. No bootstrap was rerun. Native has 246 cases and 738 repetitions, including exactly 42 Full baselines counted once. Generic completed-task measurements cover 204 split cases, 612 repetitions and 612,000 completed tasks. Energy covers the same 246 Native cases with 738 valid repetitions. The twelve short Generic predecessor measurements are a separate inventory, not an additional completed-task cohort.

2. **Generic and Native throughput are distinct and case-dependent.** For the 204 matched completed-task cases, the descriptive Native/Generic ratio ranges from 0.865 to 9.129; 62 cases have a ratio below one. These are case ratios, not a pooled cross-hardware effect estimate. The full case table retains all three-repetition medians, ranges and sample standard deviations. Historical Generic split measurements stop at logits/P2 output (499 observations); their 28 Generic Full observations already have completed endpoints. Raw-output and completed-task FPS are never relabelled as the same quantity. No per-task latency is inferred from inverse throughput.

3. **Rank transfer is not uniformly reliable.** The nine classification groups contain 17, 19 or 20 candidates and have Spearman correlations from −0.456 to 0.995. RegNet/H10 has ρ=−0.456, τ=−0.421 and 240.23% Native-cycle regret when selecting Generic Top-1. ResNet/H10 has ρ=0.489 and 108.98% regret. Conversely, MobileNet/H8 has ρ=0.995 and a correct Top-1. These differences remain within exact model/setup/precision/endpoint strata; no pooled rank correlation is reported.

4. **Completed-task measurements can change a proxy ranking without guaranteeing improvement.** On the same 20 RegNet/H8 cases, the historical raw-output proxy has ρ=0.392 and 70.73% Top-1 regret; completed-task measurements give ρ=0.941 and 8.74% regret. MobileNet/H10 remains weak (raw ρ=0.341, completed ρ=0.382), while ResNet/H8 Top-1 regret increases from 1.94% to 16.17%. The changed endpoint is observable, but timing dates, runner implementation and uncontrolled runtime conditions prevent attributing all differences solely to task completion.

5. **The YOLO addition closes a coverage gap, with very small samples.** Before augmentation, only 3 of 12 YOLO model/setup groups reach the minimum of three candidates; afterward all 12 do. Every final YOLO group contains exactly n=3. Technical Top-1 hits occur in 7/12 groups; the largest observed regret is 112.59% for YOLOv7/H8. Top-3 at n=3 is tautological. These selected development candidates are not an independent hold-out. The prepared-input H8/b066 measurement uses its actual current engine and postprocessing; it is not a historical paper-engine timing result.

6. **Valid quality loss remains visible.** Seventeen of the 204 Generic completed-task pairs carry `accuracy_loss`; all are retained in the technical view. There are 201 quality-transfer-eligible pairs, distinct from the 204 technically eligible pairs. The three first-starter pairs retain historical endpoint/semantic gate exclusions despite both runners' `reference_close` accuracy decisions. Their exact reasons are in `inputs/quality_transfer_exclusions.csv`. The separately marked `reference_close` ranking also requires the existing quality-transfer gates; it does not backfill excluded candidates. For example, MobileNet/H10 changes from n=17, ρ=0.382 to n=9, ρ=−0.533 in that restricted view.

7. **Energy uses its own measured workcounts.** Primary values are calibrated full-system energy, with no idle subtraction. Every repetition is recomputed as J/task = measured joules / actual completed workcount, watts = joules / actual active window, and energy-window throughput = actual completed workcount / actual active window. Requested workloads are 60-s screening measurements; the recorded primary windows range from 61.280 to 63.552 s. Pareto tables use only compatible model/setup/precision/endpoint/calibration/window strata and distinguish all valid quality observations from `reference_close`. Full baselines are not silently inserted into split Pareto fronts.

8. **Physical attempts are not logical repetitions.** Original budget chains independently establish 705 base starts = 702 valid + 3 completed invalid. The addition has 42 starts = 36 valid + 4 completed invalid + 2 interrupted/unconfirmed. The separate successful setup diagnostic is excluded from scientific repetition counts. These unsuccessful attempts remain evidence; none was reclassified as a valid measurement or erased by continuation.

9. **Local Split↔Full semantics are partly, not universally, established.** The base contributes 372 semantically supported comparisons out of 384 descriptive pairs. The additional stored-output replay supports 20 of 24 new comparisons, yielding 392/408 in the explicit augmented projection. The remaining four new limits are two YOLO11 vendor-Full paths with different decoder placement and two genuine YOLO26s vendor-Full numerical negatives (9/14 matches below the existing 0.8 threshold). The 12 historical limits remain. Original flags are untouched; the derived projection records its provenance, and all scientific claim flags remain false.

All numeric tables and figures regenerate offline from the compact inputs. Across 2,172 arithmetic checks, the largest absolute discrepancy is 4.95×10⁻⁷ (stored rounding); individual checks are retained. The rank code is copied verbatim from the existing project functions with function SHA provenance. Three timing repetitions quantify local variation only; they do not justify general confidence about deployment conditions.
