# Energy and calibration evidence

The three existing M.2 idle JSON files are retained as historical baselines.
They predate the final v2.79.16 actual-current full-system gain workflow and
therefore are not final thesis calibration values.

After completing the guided full-system calibration on each setup, collect the
new calibration evidence and then repeat M.2 idle calibration. The new records
should be added alongside, not silently substituted for, the historical files.
